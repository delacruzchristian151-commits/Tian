📋 Housing Loan Requirements

🇵🇭 Locally Employed

  • 2 Valid Government Issued IDs
  • Latest Certificate of Employment (COE) with Salary
  • 3 Months Latest Payslips
  • Proof of Billing (Meralco/Water Bill)
  • 2x2 ID Photos & Birth Certificate / Marriage Contract

✈️ Overseas Filipino Worker (OFW)

  • Valid Passport & Working Visa / Residence Card
  • Latest Job Contract verified by POEA / POLO
  • Proof of Remittance (3 Months)
  • Special Power of Attorney (SPA) if representative in PH
  • Proof of Billing in PH

💼 Self-Employed / Business Owners

  • DTI / SEC Business Registration
  • Audited Financial Statements (2 Years)
  • Latest Income Tax Return (ITR) Form 1701
  • 6 Months Bank Statements
  • Mayor's Permit / Business Permit
